# Go/No-Go Gates

These gates prevent syntax compliance or an attractive visualization from being mistaken for a
machine-language result.

## Gate A: Experimental Integrity

Required:

- all five primary seeds attempted for every primary variant,
- shared hashed split manifests,
- matched budgets documented,
- no-bypass tests passing,
- failures retained and reported,
- no primary metric selected after results are seen.

Failure: repair the experiment. Do not interpret model comparisons.

## Gate B: Predictive Sufficiency

The emergent graph system should finish within 10% relative performance of the continuous JEPA
ceiling on the preregistered in-distribution task aggregate.

Failure: the tested discrete channel is not a viable persistent world-model state. Investigate channel
capacity or quantization, but do not claim a language advantage.

## Gate C: Compositional Advantage

Across the primary seeds, the emergent graph must outperform the flat quantized baseline on the
preregistered held-out-composition aggregate. The paired confidence interval for the mean difference
must exclude zero.

At least one advantage must remain after normalizing by transmitted bits or training examples.

Failure: graph structure has not earned its complexity. Prefer the simpler code.

## Gate D: Causal Mediation

Required:

- removing or shuffling the bottleneck materially degrades performance,
- syntax-preserving semantic edits change behavior in the environment-predicted direction,
- injection of the correct state recovers a majority of the loss caused by corruption,
- sham edits of equal size do not produce the same directed effect.

Failure: the representation may be a label, hash, or post-hoc explanation rather than a causal
reasoning state.

## Gate E: Transmission

A fresh receiver trained only through the declared channel must learn the protocol more efficiently
than a receiver using the flat quantized baseline, measured under an equal example and bit budget.

Failure: the protocol may be sender-private rather than language-like.

## Decisions

### Full Go

Gates A-E pass. Proceed to replication, a third domain, larger capacity, cross-modal encoders, and
human-readable naming.

### Conditional Go

Integrity, sufficiency, and causal mediation pass, but graph structure or transmission does not.
Continue as discrete world-model research; suspend claims about emergent language.

### Pivot

Continuous prediction works, but every discrete structured system loses to a flat code. Reframe Limn
as a human-authored interface or analysis layer, not the model's native predictive state.

### Stop

The bottleneck is bypassed, interventions are non-causal, or results cannot be reproduced. Publish
the negative result and end the strong machine-language claim for this regime.
