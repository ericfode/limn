# Thesis

## Central Hypothesis

A sparse, discrete, graph-structured predictive bottleneck can retain most of the task-relevant
performance of a continuous world model while improving systematic generalization, communication to
new agents, channel efficiency, and causal inspectability.

This is a hypothesis, not a premise.

## What Limn Means Here

In Limn Zero, "Limn" initially means the declared persistent semantic bottleneck. It does not mean:

- the historical vocabulary,
- three-letter spellings,
- six predetermined operators,
- natural-language-like strings, or
- a claim about subjective internal thought.

A human-readable surface may be assigned only after stable predictive structures are demonstrated.

## Claim Ladder

Claims must be labeled at the strongest level actually supported:

1. **Rendered:** a decoder can describe an unrestricted latent state using Limn-like symbols.
2. **Decodable:** the bottleneck contains enough information for a probe to recover relevant factors.
3. **Exclusive:** no persistent task-relevant channel bypasses the declared bottleneck.
4. **Causally mediated:** semantic interventions on the bottleneck predictably change behavior.
5. **Compositionally advantageous:** the bottleneck beats matched alternative codes on held-out
   compositions, sample efficiency, or transmission.

Only levels 4 and 5 support the project's strong thesis.

## Design Principles

- Ground symbols in prediction and action, not English glosses.
- Predict semantic targets rather than reconstructing irrelevant surface detail.
- Require coverage of task-relevant factors through multi-target and counterfactual objectives.
- Prefer reusable relations over memorized situation IDs.
- Separate the machine representation from its eventual human-readable names.
- Measure information in bits and successful behavior, not aesthetic compactness.
- Treat interpretability as a causal property, not a visualization.

## Strong Falsifiers

The strong thesis is falsified for the tested regime if any of these persist after reasonable tuning:

- The discrete system cannot approach the continuous ceiling.
- A flat random code matches the graph bottleneck on compositional transfer.
- Removing or shuffling the bottleneck does not materially impair behavior.
- Semantic interventions do not produce the predicted behavioral changes.
- Fresh receivers cannot learn the protocol without access to sender-specific hidden state.
- Apparent concepts fail to replicate across seeds, tasks, or embodiments.

Failure does not imply the historical Limn notation is useless. It means the tested architecture does
not establish a uniquely advantageous machine-native cognitive language.
